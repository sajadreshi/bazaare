About Bazaare

Bazaare is a small online boutique for jewellery. We pick every piece for its craft, its materials and the story behind it, from everyday rings and earrings to statement necklaces made for special occasions. Many of our pieces are handmade by independent artisans, and we always tell you what each one is made of so you can choose with confidence. We are starting with jewellery and will be adding apparel soon.
