Monthly rate update

  1. Download the latest free rate table for each state you collect in from Avalara's tax rate tables ↗ (a CSV, updated monthly).
  2. Upload it here. It replaces that state's ZIP rates.
Other CSVs work too if they have zip, state and rate columns (rate as 0.08625 or 8.625).
